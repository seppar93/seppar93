## 👋 About me

I'm a Staff Software Engineer in Toronto with 8+ years of experience, most recently in fintech building retirement-income platforms. I like the hard middle of software: taking an ambiguous problem, shaping the architecture, and staying with it until it's reliable in production.

**How I work**
- 🧭 **Set direction:** I write RFCs and technical strategy, and turn them into roadmaps teams can execute.
- 🏛️ **Own the architecture:** I've been the lead architect on multiple platforms, from the first design to production.
- 🔒 **Raise the bar:** security-first design, fail-closed defaults, and quality gates in CI.
- 🤝 **Multiply the team:** code review, mentoring, and AI tooling that makes every engineer faster.
